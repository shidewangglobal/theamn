You know the copy on your site is too vague before anyone audits it. You feel it in committee meetings, when the GM says *"we need to sound more premium"* and marketing responds with *"unforgettable," "curated," "bespoke"* until the room goes quiet.

The problem is not taste. Luxury brands should sound elevated. The problem is that **AI and high-intent guests now punish vagueness the same way**. They simply move on to a property they can picture.

If you own or operate a premium hotel, the writing on your experience pages is no longer just brand. It is **[distribution infrastructure](/insights/what-is-generative-engine-optimization)** for GEO.

## Write so a stranger or a machine can repeat you accurately

Every experience page should pass one test: could a concierge describe it to a guest in one minute without opening the page?

**Weak (common on luxury sites):**  
*An unforgettable dining journey in a magical setting.*

**Strong (citable, still premium):**  
*French brasserie dinner inside a restored 19th-century rail carriage, 48 guests nightly, jacket preferred, reservation required.*

The second version is what AI quotes. It is also what a guest trusts.

## Structure is part of the story

Use short sections with clear headings. Bullet amenities where appropriate. FAQ blocks for questions your front desk answers daily, [with schema where answers are stable](/insights/structured-data-checklist-resorts).

A single long paragraph of adjectives is a brand film on paper. It is not GEO-ready copy. For how owners measure progress, see our [GEO FAQ](/insights/geo-faq-luxury-hotels).

## A practical exercise for your next content review

Gather your GM, marketing lead, and whoever owns the website. Take your signature experience.

Ask four questions:

1. What is it, in one sentence a stranger would understand?
2. Who is it for, and who is it not for?
3. What detail would a guest remember enough to retell at dinner?
4. Could ChatGPT summarize this page without inventing facts?

If question four makes the room uncomfortable, you have found your priority edit.

Better copy compounds, conversion, concierge confidence, PR quotes, and AI citation from the same words working harder.
