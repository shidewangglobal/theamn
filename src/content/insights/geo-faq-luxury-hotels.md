Every month, another owner or GM asks us the same thing in slightly different words: *"We rank on Google. Why doesn't ChatGPT know we exist?"*

Fair question. You have paid for SEO. You have a beautiful site. Your reviews are strong. And yet AI answers feel like they were written for a parallel universe where your property never opened.

You are not behind because you ignored digital marketing. You are behind because **discovery moved**, and most luxury portfolios still measure success with tools built for the old funnel.

In luxury hospitality, the guests with the highest lifetime value are often the first to change how they research. They ask AI for a shortlist before they ask a travel advisor. GEO is not about chasing trends. It is about making sure the property you built is **findable** when intent is real, and **described accurately** when you are found.

The questions below are the ones we hear in owner meetings, asset reviews, and marketing audits. Each answer is short on purpose. Use them to brief your team, challenge your agency, or sanity-check whether your current SEO retainer actually covers generative discovery. New to GEO? Start with [what Generative Engine Optimization is](/insights/what-is-generative-engine-optimization).

## Is GEO just *SEO with a new name*?

No. SEO optimizes for position on a search results page. GEO optimizes for being referenced inside AI-generated answers, which requires entity clarity, structured content, and authoritative signals beyond rankings alone.

## How long before we see *results from GEO work*?

Technical fixes can show movement in weeks. Competitive luxury markets usually need three to six months of sustained content and citation building. AI models refresh faster than traditional search, but only if your owned presence gives them something accurate to cite.

## Do we need *separate content written for AI*?

No robotic microsites. You need [clear, specific, honest copy](/insights/experience-copy-ai-can-cite) about what makes your property distinct, structured so machines can parse it without losing your brand voice.

## Can we *measure GEO performance*?

Yes. Run a baseline GEO audit, then track branded and category queries in AI tools monthly, monitor AI referral traffic, and check whether third-party mentions align with your owned narrative.

## Who on our team *should own GEO*?

Ideally a partnership between the owner or GM (standards), marketing (voice and content), and web or agency partners ([schema and crawl](/insights/structured-data-checklist-resorts)). Without an executive sponsor, GEO becomes a slide deck instead of a revenue discipline.

If several answers feel uncomfortably familiar, that is useful data. It usually means the fix is foundational: entity clarity, experience copy, technical access, not another campaign.
