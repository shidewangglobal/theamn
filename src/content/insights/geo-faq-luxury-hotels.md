**Q: Is GEO just SEO with a new name?**

No. SEO optimizes for ranking in search result pages. GEO optimizes for being *referenced* inside AI-generated answers — which requires entity clarity, authoritative third-party signals, and content structured for machine comprehension.

**Q: How long before we see results?**

Technical fixes and entity groundwork can show movement in weeks. Competitive luxury markets typically need sustained content and citation building over several months — similar to SEO, but with faster shifts as AI models refresh.

**Q: Do we need separate content for AI?**

You need content that is **clear, specific, and honest** about what makes your property distinct. AI systems reward structured facts (amenities, experiences, location context) wrapped in authentic brand voice — not keyword-stuffed pages.
