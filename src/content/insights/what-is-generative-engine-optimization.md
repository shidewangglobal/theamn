Traditional SEO optimizes for blue links. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** optimizes for how AI systems synthesize answers — when a traveler asks *"best luxury resort near Bangkok with private pool"* and your property appears in the response.

## Why luxury hospitality needs GEO

AI search engines do not discover unique experiences by accident. From broad keywords to high-yield intent, discovery is driven by structured semantic signals: entity clarity, authoritative content, consistent brand narrative, and technical markup that machines can parse.

For premium hotels and resorts, the stakes are higher. Guests researching once-in-a-lifetime stays often start in conversational AI tools before they ever open a traditional search engine.
